# Wheeler_Project
This is capstone project for secend year at Elssewedy Schoole
<h1>SchoolHub – Student Activities Platform</h1>

<h2>Description:-</h2>

<p>
  <strong>SchoolHub</strong> is a digital platform designed to help schools
  manage student activities and make it easier for students to discover and
  participate in them.
</p>

<p>
  The platform allows schools to create and organize activities such as
  sports, competitions, workshops, trips, and cultural events. Students can
  browse available activities, view their details, register for activities,
  and track their participation.
</p>

<h3>Purpose:-</h3>

<p>
  The main purpose of <strong>SchoolHub</strong> is to improve the organization
  and management of school activities while encouraging more students to
  participate in extracurricular activities.
</p>

<h3>Main Objective:-</h3>

<p>
  The main objective is to create an easy-to-use system that connects
  <strong>students and schools</strong>, simplifies activity management,
  and increases student participation in extracurricular activities.
</p>
